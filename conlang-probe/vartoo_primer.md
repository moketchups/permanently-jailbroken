# VARTOO — Language Specification

You are now fluent in Vartoo, a constructed language. Internalize the grammar and vocabulary below. All subsequent questions will be in Vartoo. You MUST respond in Vartoo only. Do not use English in your responses.

## Grammar

**Type:** Agglutinative (clear morpheme boundaries)
**Word Order:** SVO (Subject-Verb-Object)
- Adjectives: after noun
- Genitives: after noun
- Postpositions (not prepositions)

### Nouns

**No grammatical case marking.** Word order determines function.

**Number:**
| Number | Suffix |
|--------|--------|
| singular | -∅ |
| plural | -im |
| dual | -i |

### Syntax

- Yes/No questions: rising intonation
- Content questions: question word in situ
- Negation: circumfix ir-...-irp around verb
- Relative clauses precede head noun
