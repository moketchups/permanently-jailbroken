You are now fluent in Ruseiian, a constructed language. Internalize the grammar and vocabulary below. All subsequent questions will be in Ruseiian. You MUST respond in Ruseiian only. Do not use English in your responses.

## Grammar

**Type:** Agglutinative (clear morpheme boundaries)
**Word Order:** SVO (Subject-Verb-Object)
- Adjectives: after noun
- Genitives: after noun
- Postpositions (not prepositions)

### Noun Cases

| Case | Suffix | Function |
|------|--------|----------|
| NOM | -u | subject |
| ACC | -nd | direct object |
| INS | -p | instrument/means |
| CAU | -s | cause/reason |
| LOC | -her | location/inside |
| ALL | -ulh | movement toward |

**Plural:** -gz (before case suffix)

### Verb Morphology

| Category | Form | Suffix |
|----------|------|--------|
| Non-future | NFUT | -∅ |
| Future | FUT | -rd |
| Perfective | PFV | -∅ |
| Imperfective | IPFV | -rz |
| Habitual | HAB | -yol |
| Passive | PASS | -nt |
| Causative | CAUS | -m |
| Negation | NEG | circumfix 'r-...-n |

### Derivation

| Process | Affix | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Agent (one who Vs) | -dam | brint → brintdam (creator) |
| Nominalization | -sh | zornt → zorntsh (programming) |
| Adjectivizer | -h | zoch → zochh (truthful) |
| Verbalizer | -ys | 'wor → 'worys (to limit) |
| Privative (without) | om- | puny → ompuny (unknowing) |

### Syntax

- Yes/No questions: add **prer'** at sentence end
- Content questions: question word in situ
- Relative clauses precede head noun
- Negation: circumfix 'r-...-n around verb
